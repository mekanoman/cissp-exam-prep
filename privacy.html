Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. CISSP Exam Prep is published by Bintera Technologies.

The short version

We don't collect any information about you. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your study progress

The lessons you finish, where you stopped and your playback settings are saved on your device. If you use iCloud, the app also saves them to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac stay in step. That data belongs to your Apple Account; Bintera Technologies can't see or access it. To remove it, delete the app from each device, or turn off iCloud for the app in your device settings.

Purchases

The one-time course unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support requests

Support requests are made through a form on GitHub, and they are public. Please don't include personal information in them. We use what you write only to answer you.

Children

The app is a professional study course and isn't directed at children. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: send a support request.
