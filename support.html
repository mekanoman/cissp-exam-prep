Support

Help with CISSP Exam Prep by Bintera on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact us

Email support@bintera.com. Tell us your device and what happened, and we'll reply within two business days.

You can also send a support request on GitHub. Those requests are public, so please don't include personal information there.

I bought the full course on another device

Open any lesson from Lesson 5 on, then tap Restore Purchases. On a Mac you can also choose CISSP Exam Prep › Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

My progress isn't on my other device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and open the app on each one. Changes usually arrive within a minute while both devices are online.

I can't hear the narration

Turn the volume up. The narration plays with the silent switch on, and keeps playing when the screen locks. Use the lock screen or Control Center to pause or skip lines.

What's free?

All of Domain 1, Security and Risk Management (Lessons 1–4). One purchase unlocks Domains 2 to 8 on all your devices. There's no subscription.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
