Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Bintera CISSP® Exam Prep is published by Bintera Technologies.

The short version

We don't collect any information about you. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your study progress

Your study progress is saved on your device: the lessons you finish and where you stopped, practice scores and history, your streak and badges, playback settings, your Certificate of Completion record (its number and date), and the name and photo you add to your profile or certificate. If you use iCloud, the app also saves them to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac stay in step. That data belongs to your Apple Account; Bintera Technologies can't see or access it. To remove your study progress from all your devices, use Reset all progress in the app's Help or on the Progress screen (your name, photo and purchase stay). To remove your name and photo, open the Progress screen, clear the name field and tap Remove next to the photo; the change reaches your other devices too. You can also stop syncing by turning off iCloud for the app in your device settings.

On Android, progress isn't synced between devices as you study. It is included in your phone's own Android backup and device-to-device transfer, so a new phone can keep it; that backup belongs to your Google account and Bintera Technologies can't see or access it. Reset all progress clears progress on that device, and the name and photo are removed the same way.

Purchases

The one-time course unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. On Android, the unlock is processed by Google Play, and Google's handling is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Support messages

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete the conversation when it's finished. Support requests sent through GitHub are public, so please don't include personal information in them.

Children

The app is a professional study course and isn't directed at children. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
